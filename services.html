Services

Five disciplines, each booked and priced differently. Get in touch for a tailored quote — every session includes a private online proof gallery once your shoot is edited.

01 / Landscape

Landscape & Fine Art

Location prints and commissioned landscape work, shot on request at a specific site, time of day or season.

  • Bespoke location shoots
  • Fine-art print commissions
  • Licensing for print & editorial
02 / Animal

Wildlife & Pet Portraits

Patient, long-lens work for wild subjects, plus on-location portrait sessions for pets and working animals.

  • Wildlife hide & field sessions
  • Pet & equine portraits
  • Farm & estate animal documentation
03 / Sport

Sport & Action

Match-day and event coverage for clubs, schools and individual athletes, with fast turnaround for press use.

  • Match & tournament coverage
  • Individual athlete portfolios
  • Same-week digital delivery
04 / Portraiture

Portrait Sessions

Studio or on-location portraits for individuals, couples and families, in colour or black and white.

  • Studio & outdoor sessions
  • Family & couples portraits
  • Professional headshots
05 / Commercial

Commercial & Brand

Product, interior and staff photography for small businesses that need images they actually own the rights to.

  • Product & hospitality shoots
  • Interiors & workspace photography
  • Staff & team headshots
06 / Custom

Something else?

If your project doesn't fit neatly into a category above, tell me about it — most bookings start as a conversation.

  • Editorial & documentary
  • Events & exhibitions
  • Collaborations & commissions
