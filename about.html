About

I got into photography because I couldn't sit still.

Fifteen years ago that meant hiking before sunrise to catch the light on a ridge. These days it's just as likely to mean sitting in a hide for four hours waiting on a barn owl, or standing pitchside in the rain for a Sunday league final. The common thread is patience — waiting for the frame that actually says something, rather than settling for the one that's merely in focus.

I work across five areas — landscape, animal, sport, portraiture and commercial — and each one teaches me something the others don't. Landscape work built my patience. Sport sharpened my timing. Portraiture taught me to read people quickly. I bring all three to every job now, whatever it says on the booking form.
